Technical overview

Paper-trade the S&P 500.
Engineered like a trading desk.

Players manage a virtual portfolio of real S&P 500 names and compete on a perpetual leaderboard. Quotes and historical OHLCV bars are ingested from external REST providers into TimescaleDB, the single source of truth for every fill, valuation, and ranking. TypeScript end to end, with contracts shared across the wire.
